Questions

Quick answers.

How fast is delivery?

Most orders are fulfilled within minutes of payment. Once your payment is confirmed, use the Redeem page with your invoice ID to pick a delivery method.

Where do I find my invoice ID?

It's in your purchase confirmation after checkout. Copy it exactly as shown and paste it on the Redeem page.

What does the stock number mean?

It's the quantity currently available from our store. When a product reaches zero it shows as sold out until it's restocked.

Something went wrong with my order.

Message us on Telegram with your invoice ID and we'll sort it out.
